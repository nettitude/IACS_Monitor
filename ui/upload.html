Every upload belongs to one site (plant); its data is kept separate from other sites.
Log lines are stored in the site of the device that sent them (device registry: Devices page of the console). Lines from unknown devices are held as unmapped unless a site is picked above — it maps unknown devices only and never overrides an existing mapping (lines of a device registered to another site are held as conflicts).
