Capture point
Naming where the capture was taken lets a single routed frame identify the firewall's MAC, so a host behind it is never recorded with the firewall's MAC. Without it the capture is still ingested (flagged unassociated) and can be associated later below.
Each log line is stored in the site of the device that sent it (see Devices). Lines from unknown devices are held as unmapped unless you pick a site here — it maps unknown devices only and never overrides an existing mapping (lines of a device registered to another site are held as conflicts for you to resolve).
Devices

Registered devices

Log lines are stored in the site of the device that sent them (matched by serial, then by log hostname / devname). Firewall config uploads register their hostname automatically. Remapping a device affects new log lines only.

Conflicts

Lines that collide with the registry are held here instead of being routed by guess — no checks run on them. Serial conflict: the line's serial differs from the serial registered for that device name. Site mismatch: an upload named another site than the one the device is registered to. HA member candidate: a new serial under the name of a device registered as an HA cluster — Add as HA member if it is the other cluster unit (members evidenced by an HA event log from a known member are added automatically). Choose what the lines are: the registered device (e.g. a replaced unit), a different device at a site you pick, or discard them. Resolving replays the held lines into that site and applies to new lines; stored data is never moved.

Unmapped devices

Lines from devices that are not mapped to any site are held here — no checks run on them. Map a device to replay its held lines into that site, or discard them. Held lines are purged after the retention period (IACS_UNMAPPED_RETENTION_DAYS, default 30 days).

Report details

Select a site at the top left.

Periodic reviews

Runs all periodic ISA/IEC 62443 reviews now (otherwise they run on the server schedule). Findings no longer reproduced are auto-resolved; repeated ones are escalated.

Checks in the report

Every check of the monitor, grouped. Untick a group or a check to leave it out of this site's compliance report; the selection is saved for the site. The findings of a check left out are hidden throughout the application (Findings page, counts, badges, exports) - the Findings page can show them on request - and the check keeps being evaluated, so putting it back shows the current state. The report lists the groups and checks left out, with the reason.

Audit checklist report (xlsx → PDF / CSV)

Upload the filled-in ISA/IEC 62443 Master Audit Checklist workbook (sheet Master Checklist; the yellow columns Status, Maturity Level, Finding / Observation, Evidence Reviewed, Severity, Recommendation, Owner, Target Date). Each checklist row becomes one report finding: the check title is the finding's title, the audit check its details, with the domain and your observation, evidence and recommendation as written. The PDF uses the LRQA report layout and this site's Report details; the CSV holds the same findings, one line each. Nothing is stored.

Pre-filled from this site's Report details when they are set; what you enter here is used for this report only (nothing is saved).
